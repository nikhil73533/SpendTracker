Understand your everyday spending with TrackMint, a personal money tracker that brings income, expenses and account activity together.

TRACK NEW TRANSACTIONS
Choose SMS tracking to detect financial transactions from new messages. Tracking starts when you enable it; older inbox messages are not automatically imported. SMS parsing happens on your device. You can decline SMS access and add transactions manually.

KEEP YOUR MONEY ORGANIZED
Add and edit income and expenses, assign categories, review account activity, bookmark transactions and organize related entries. Browse daily and monthly views to understand where your money goes.

SEE YOUR SPENDING CLEARLY
Explore charts, category breakdowns and trends. Compare periods, review income and expenses, and use budgets to keep track of your plans.

IMPORT AND EXPORT
Import supported PDF bank statements, review the extracted transactions, and export reports for your own records. Recognition accuracy depends on the statement format; review imported amounts and categories before relying on them.

TOOLS FOR EVERYDAY USE
Use bill reminders, account histories, transaction groups and a built-in calculator. Keep control of your records with manual corrections and trash recovery.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Core financial processing works on your device. Your local transaction database is encrypted. Android backup follows your device backup settings. Phone backups use a folder and password you choose. You control exports and sharing.

NO CHECKOUT IN THIS VERSION
The Google Play launch version includes its features without a paid checkout. No TrackMint account is required for on-device features. Android cloud recovery uses your own Google account when device backup is enabled.

TrackMint is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not move money or provide banking services.

REINSTALL RECOVERY
With Android backup enabled, reinstalling can restore your last completed backup. You can also save password-protected copies of records and the learning model to a folder you choose. After reinstall, select a saved file and enter its password. Backup timing and availability depend on Android; keep a phone copy too.
