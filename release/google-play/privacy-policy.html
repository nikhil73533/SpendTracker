TrackMint Privacy Policy

About this policy

TrackMint provides the TrackMint Android application. This policy describes the Google Play version with paid checkout disabled. Effective date: 19 October 2026. For privacy questions, contact ng1200821@gmail.com.

Information processed on your device

The app processes transactions you enter or import, amounts, categories, dates, account labels, descriptions, budgets, bill reminders, and optional profile details you save. It uses this information to organize records, identify transactions, categorize spending and display reports. The local transaction database is encrypted. Local preferences, exported files and backups may have different protection; protect your device and files.

SMS access and your choice

If you allow SMS access, TrackMint reads incoming messages and checks the inbox for financial messages received after tracking was first enabled. This supports transaction detection, bill reminders and recovery of messages missed while the app was closed. A fresh installation does not automatically import older inbox messages. SMS content is processed on the device and is not sent to a TrackMint server for parsing. Work queued for background processing can contain message content locally. You can decline or revoke SMS permission in Android settings and continue entering transactions manually.

Documents, images and exports

When you select a statement or receipt, the app processes the selected content to extract transaction details. PDF text recognition and bundled OCR run on the device. If you export or share a report, the selected data is copied to the location or app you choose. The recipient or service then handles that copy under its own terms.

Backup and recovery

When Android backup is enabled in your device settings and supports client-side encryption, Android may back up the finance database, learning model and recovery key to your Google account. Supported device-to-device transfers can also carry this data. These copies let Android restore records after reinstall. Android controls timing, availability and its 25 MB quota; the latest changes may not have been backed up. No SMS inbox or sign-in credentials are included in this recovery snapshot.

You can also choose a folder and password for encrypted phone backup files. Each copy includes records, the learning model and the database recovery key, protected together with password-based encryption. Daily phone backups are optional. The selected folder may be on your phone or a document provider you choose; that provider handles stored files under its own terms. A protected password copy is kept in this installation for backups and excluded from Android backup. After reinstall, choose a saved file and enter the original password. We cannot reset this password. Files in the chosen folder survive uninstall and must be deleted separately.

Other online services and SDKs

Opening help or feedback links loads an external website in your browser. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Bundled ML Kit OCR collects device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes and feature events for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states this telemetry is encrypted in transit. Google Play services, reviews and Google sign-in also process information as described in Google policies. This Play version does not offer Razorpay checkout or collect a payment through TrackMint. We do not sell your SMS or financial records.

Permissions

SMS permissions enable optional automatic transaction tracking. Notification permission enables reminders. Internet access supports optional online services. File access is limited to app storage and documents or destinations you select. Android lets you manage permissions in system settings.

Retention and deletion

On-device records remain until you remove them or clear the app storage. Moving a transaction to Trash retains it until permanently deleted. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-private data; files you exported, phone backup files and Android backup copies can remain. Delete saved files through the Files app and manage Android backup copies in your device or Google account backup settings. An available Android backup may restore records when you reinstall. To request deletion of information you sent to support, email ng1200821@gmail.com. The Play version does not require a separate TrackMint account.

Security and children

We use local database encryption and password-based authenticated encryption for phone backups and require protected Android backup transports. No device or transfer method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and do not share backup archives publicly. TrackMint is intended for general personal money management and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The effective date will change with an update. Developer/business name: TrackMint. Support and privacy contact: ng1200821@gmail.com.
